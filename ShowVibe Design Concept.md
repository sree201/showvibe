# ShowVibe Design Concept

## 1. Core Principles
- **User-Centric:** Intuitive and easy-to-use interface for all age groups.
- **Unified Experience:** Seamless integration of movie, event, and restaurant booking.
- **Visually Appealing:** Modern, clean, and engaging design.
- **Efficient:** Streamlined booking process with minimal steps.
- **Reliable:** Clear feedback and secure transactions.

## 2. Color Palette
Inspired by the vibrant and dynamic nature of entertainment and dining, the color palette will be modern and inviting.
- **Primary Color (Accent):** `#FF6B6B` (A vibrant coral/red, symbolizing excitement and energy)
- **Secondary Color (Action/Highlight):** `#4ECDC4` (A refreshing teal, representing clarity and efficiency)
- **Neutral Colors (Backgrounds/Text):**
    - `#F7F7F7` (Light grey, for clean backgrounds)
    - `#333333` (Dark grey, for primary text and strong contrasts)
    - `#666666` (Medium grey, for secondary text and subtle elements)

## 3. Typography
Readability and modern aesthetics are key. A sans-serif font family will be used for its versatility and clean appearance.
- **Headings:** Montserrat (Bold and impactful for titles and important information)
- **Body Text:** Open Sans (Highly readable and clean for general content)

## 4. Layout and Structure
The app will feature a tab-based navigation for easy switching between Movies, Events, and Restaurants. Each section will have a consistent card-based layout for listings, allowing for quick scanning of information.

- **Home Screen:** Personalized recommendations and quick access to popular categories.
- **Listing Pages (Movies, Events, Restaurants):**
    - Card-based display with clear images, titles, dates/times, and locations.
    - Filtering and sorting options at the top.
    - Search bar for specific queries.
- **Detail Pages:**
    - Large hero image/video for visual appeal.
    - Comprehensive information (synopsis, cast, menu, event details).
    - Clear call-to-action for booking/reservation.
    - Integrated map for location-based services.
- **Booking/Reservation Flow:**
    - Multi-step form with clear progress indicators.
    - Seat/table selection (where applicable).
    - Summary of selection before payment.
- **Payment Integration:**
    - Secure and intuitive payment gateway interface.
    - Support for multiple payment methods.
- **User Profile:**
    - Booking history.
    - Saved preferences.
    - Account settings.

## 5. Iconography
Clean, modern, and easily recognizable icons will be used throughout the application to enhance usability and visual appeal. Icons will be flat or subtly outlined.

## 6. Responsiveness
The design will be fully responsive, adapting seamlessly to various screen sizes (mobile, tablet, desktop) to ensure a consistent user experience across all devices.

## 7. Accessibility
Considerations for accessibility will be integrated, including sufficient color contrast, clear typography, and proper labeling for screen readers.
